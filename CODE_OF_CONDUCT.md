# Community Code of Conduct | قواعد السلوك

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code, explain disagreements and help others learn.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sharing private credentials or customer information, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. These expectations cover repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related community spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, request changes, or restrict participation. Responses should consider impact, context and repeated behavior. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through a verified private contact on their GitHub profile; do not expose affected people in public reports. If the maintainer is involved, contact another available project maintainer privately. Do not post personal details in issues.

نرحب بالجميع. احترم الآخرين، وناقش الأفكار بأدب، واحمِ الخصوصية. التحرش والتمييز والتهديد ونشر البيانات الخاصة غير مقبولة. أبلغ المسؤولين عبر قناة خاصة موثوقة.
